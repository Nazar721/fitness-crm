**Root cause**: The `main` element has `lg:pl-20` (80px left padding) and the inner container has `max-w-6xl mx-auto`. On the exercises page, the content appears to start AT the sidebar edge instead of after it — exercise names like "ВІДЖИМАННЯ" are clipped to "ЖИМАННЯ".

**Fix**: Restructure the layout so the content properly accounts for the sidebar width. Change from `pl-20 + mx-auto` to a proper approach:
- File: `src/app/layout.tsx`
- Change: Replace `lg:pl-20` with a sidebar-aware layout. The inner container's `mx-auto` should work within the space left of the sidebar, not fight with it.

Approach: Keep `lg:pl-20` on main but ensure the inner `max-w-6xl mx-auto` div has `lg:ml-0` or adjust so centering doesn't push content under the sidebar. Actually the simplest fix: remove `mx-auto` on large screens and use `lg:ml-8` (32px gap from sidebar) instead, OR just add `overflow-hidden` to ensure nothing bleeds under the sidebar.

Actually, the real issue is likely that `max-w-6xl` (1152px) + `pl-20` (80px) = 1232px which on some screens causes the centered container to overlap the sidebar. Fix: change the inner container to be properly offset.

**Simplest fix**: On the `<main>`, keep `lg:pl-20`. On the inner div, change `mx-auto` to `lg:mx-0 lg:ml-auto lg:mr-auto` or simply ensure the content doesn't bleed left. Actually, the cleanest approach:
- Add `overflow-x-hidden` to the main container
- OR change `max-w-6xl mx-auto` to use `lg:pr-8` to ensure right spacing while keeping left clear

**Recommended fix**: The issue is that `mx-auto` centers the content div, but on screens where `viewport - 80px (pl-20)` is close to `max-w-6xl`, the left edge of the centered div ends up very close to the sidebar. Fix by ensuring minimum left margin:
- File: `src/app/layout.tsx` line 28
- Change: `w-full max-w-6xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8 xl:px-12` → `w-full max-w-6xl mx-auto px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8 xl:px-12 lg:ml-[max(2rem,calc((100vw-80px-72rem)/2+2rem))]` — too complex.

**Actually simplest**: The layout already has `lg:pl-20`. The issue is the `max-w-6xl mx-auto` centers within the padded area. If the padded area is ~1200px and max-w-6xl is 1152px, there's only 24px margin on each side — and the left 24px is NOT enough visual separation from the sidebar.
